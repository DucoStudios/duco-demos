Threads

Right agent.
Right thread.
Every time.

Every task gets its own thread, with its own agent and its own memory. All threads sit in one list, showing who is working, who is waiting and who is done.

Live

as it happens.

Answers stream in word by word. The flow panel shows every step the agent takes, with timing, pulsing while it runs. Tap a step to jump straight to it in the chat.

Approvals

The agents do the work.
You hold the lever.

When an agent wants to do something that needs your yes, the question pops up as a button. Allow or deny. Nothing slips past you, and you can stop an agent mid-step.

Threads can read each other. Link an earlier thread as context, or let the agent look up what you decided last week on its own, instead of guessing.
