FAQ

Before you start.

Do I have to leave Airbnb or Booking.com?

No. Stay there to be seen. DucoBook handles the guests who already know you, and the calendar lets you run every channel at the same time.

What if the same dates are booked in two places?

DucoBook checks your channels the moment a guest books with you. If a clash still happens, Airbnb wins and the guest on your site is refunded automatically. A clash between Airbnb and Booking.com is never resolved automatically: you get an email so you can decide.

Why does it take a while for Airbnb to see my direct bookings?

Airbnb and Booking.com fetch external calendars every few hours, which we don’t control. That’s why we recommend Request to Book: you can check the dates are free before anything is charged to the guest’s card.

Do I need a new website?

No. If you already have one, we add the booking box to it. If you don’t, we build one for you on your own domain.

Who owns my guests’ details?

You do. Bookings and contact details live in your portal, and if you leave we hand everything over.

What language is the portal in?

The owner portal is in English today, with Swedish and French on the way. Your site and guest emails can be in French, English and Swedish.

Do you help with tax and VAT on rentals?

The finance view gives you the figures and a file for your accountant. We don’t give tax or VAT advice: that’s a job for an accountant in your country.

Is DucoBook right for everyone?

It suits hosts with one to about ten properties. If you already have a channel manager connected to Booking.com, or more than twenty room types, Booking.com doesn’t allow calendar sync by link. If so, we’ll tell you on the first call.
